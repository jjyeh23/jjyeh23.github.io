## Project Overview

My research investigates how wildflower phenology responses under changing planetary environmental conditions. I use satellite remote sensing and ecological observations to determine variation in flowering timing across space and time.

## Research Questions

- How have the phenological events of Eschscholzia californica shifted in space and time?
- How do climate and geophysical stressors affect Eschscholzia californica phenology in an extreme and warming climate?
- Can we translate our current knowledge about climate and phenology to forecast future wildflower phenology?
- Can this knowledge be expandable to photosynthetic life detection on other planets?

## Methods

- Multi-sensor image analysis of vegetation dynamics
- Statistical machine learning to model relationships between vegetation and the environment
- Time-series analysis in phenological modeling for forecasting planetary climate regimes

## Skills Demonstrated

- Satellite remote sensing analysis
- Data processing using automated scripts and optimized pipelines
- Practical and theoretical ecology applications

## Future Directions

This project sparks my interest in vegetation and floral phenology, and investigating the spectrotemporal dynamics of plant pigment phenology in the future as a potential biosignature in planetary environments.
